01 / About

Ideas, systems and craft—
across every format.

I’m Linoy Stephen, a Senior Graphic Designer and Web Designer based in Dubai, with more than 20 years of experience across the UAE, GCC, Africa and India.

My core strength is marketing and corporate communications: brand guidelines, campaigns, presentations, brochures, exhibition collateral and corporate video. Alongside that is a specialised practice in regulated pharmaceutical packaging across Rx, OTC and consumer categories.

I translate complex information into clear, credible visual systems. My work moves comfortably from a packaging artwork and a 500-page directory to an exhibition environment, responsive website or AI-assisted campaign.

AI is part of my day-to-day creative process—not a side experiment. I use Claude, ChatGPT and Grok for ideation and briefs, and ChatGPT, Google AI, Adobe Firefly and Midjourney for image development and iteration.
